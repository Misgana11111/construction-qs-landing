BEFORE YOU PURCHASE

Frequently asked questions.

Review the practical details before opening the product listing.

What is this product?

It is an Excel-based construction measurement, quantity takeoff, BOQ and cost management workbook with connected project, measurement, takeoff, commercial and reporting worksheets.

Who is it designed for?

Quantity Surveyors, Civil Engineers, Contractors, Consultants, Site Engineers, Estimators and Project Managers involved in construction quantity and cost workflows.

Does it include specialized takeoff sheets?

Yes. The workbook includes concrete, masonry, finishes, earthwork, material takeoff and Bar Bending Schedule modules.

Does it include payment and close-out documents?

Yes. Its commercial area includes variation orders, IPC, measurement certificates, final account and related reports.

Is it customized for Ethiopia?

It is labeled Ethiopia Edition and includes an ETB currency workflow and an Ethiopian Standard Method of Measurement (ESMM) field in the supplied project setup example. Confirm your specific contract requirements before use.

How do I see the price and purchase it?

Use any “View on Ye-Buna” or “Get the System” button. The Ye-Buna product page displays the current listing details and available checkout process.

Do I need Excel experience?

Basic spreadsheet familiarity is helpful. Users should understand their measurement, BOQ and contract procedures and review workbook outputs before issuing them as project records.

Does the workbook replace professional checking?

No. It is a working tool. Users remain responsible for checking formulas, quantities, rates, contract conditions, approvals and issued documents.
